About the study

UCA stands for Understanding Cyber-Aggression through AI Use, Trust, and Personality Factors.

In one paragraph

This project explores how people’s use of AI tools, their trust in those tools, and personality-related factors may relate to cyber-aggression and online behavior. This hub tracks progress and provides high-level information suitable for the public.

What you’ll find here

  • Progress milestones (a “thermometer” view)
  • Updates (short, dated entries)
  • Ethics & privacy (including whether participation is open)
